The Guide

Ezriyah

Founder, Manbodiment

I spent a career as a Radiation Health Physicist: a field built on precision, on measuring what is invisible, on understanding what operates beneath the surface. Then I spent years discovering I had applied none of that rigour to my own life.

I have been walking men through the passage from scared to sacred since 2012. Through 1:1 sessions, group circles, retreat immersions, and the kind of work that does not come with a certificate. The men who find me are not broken. They are numb, and smart enough to know the difference matters.

My work draws on Haitian and Gabonese ancestral lineage, somatic and embodiment practice, and psychedelic integration support. I am not here to give you a framework for manhood. I am here to help you find out what yours actually is.
