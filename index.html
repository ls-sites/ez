The "Sacred," lived
The Manbodiment Method

The pillars that make an integrated man.

Every session, every practice, every piece of this work lives inside one of these three. Not completed once; returned to in spirals, each time deeper.

Presence
Fully here, not performing here. Actually here.

Presence is the ability to remain fully engaged in the current moment, undistracted by the past you cannot change or the future you cannot control.

When a man carries real presence, the people around him feel it. Rooms change, not because you have learned a technique, but because you have finally arrived.

Mental clarity Emotional receptivity Physical grounding Safety for others
Purpose
A direction that is yours, not inherited, not performed.

Purpose is the organizing principle of your choices. It tells you what to say yes to and what to release.

Most men have never stopped long enough to locate it. When you do, difficulty stops being a detour and starts being information. Your energy stops being scattered and starts being directed.

Vision clarity Values alignment Legacy orientation Decision confidence
Power
Decisive action aligned with your values, not your fear.

This is not dominance. Power is the capacity to act from your deepest truth, set clear boundaries, protect what matters, and create real impact.

It is the opposite of the low-grade aggression or quiet shutdown most men cycle between. It is what remains when you are no longer afraid of yourself.

Healthy boundaries Decisive action Disciplined practice Real impact

The Guide

Ezriyah

I spent a career as a Radiation Health Physicist: a field built on precision, on measuring what is invisible, on understanding what operates beneath the surface. Then I spent years discovering I had applied none of that rigour to my own life.

I have been walking men through the passage from scared to sacred since 2012. Through 1:1 sessions, group circles, retreat immersions, and the kind of work that does not come with a certificate. The men who find me are not broken. They are numb, and smart enough to know the difference matters.

My work draws on Haitian and Gabonese ancestral lineage, somatic and embodiment practice, and psychedelic integration support. I am not here to give you a framework for manhood. I am here to help you find out what yours actually is.

Common Questions

Questions, answered.

Is this therapy?

No. This is embodiment and integration work, not clinical treatment. If you are in crisis or working through a diagnosed condition, please also work with a licensed therapist or medical provider. Many men do both at once.

Who is this work for?

Men who look successful from the outside and feel disconnected on the inside. If you are managing a life more than living it, this is built for you.

Do I need a specific problem to start?

No. Most men arrive with a vague sense that something is off, not a diagnosis. The 7-Day Challenge is designed to help you find the shape of it.

What if I am skeptical?

Good. Bring the skepticism. The work does not ask you to believe anything, only to be honest for seven days and see what happens.

What happens after the 7-Day Challenge?

You will have a clear sense of whether the method is real for you. From there, a Soul Deep Session or the Manbodiment Intensive are the next steps, whenever you are ready.

How is the Manbodiment Intensive structured?

Eight weeks organized around Presence, Purpose, and Power, available 1:1 or in a small group of up to ten men. A discovery call comes first, to confirm it is the right fit.
