The man I work with

You don't need me to tell you something is wrong. You already know.

"There is a lot being demanded of me, but mostly by me."
"I am surrounded by people and I feel alone in the room."
"There are feelings I cannot access, and feelings I cannot get past."
"I am faking my whole life and I am tired of pretending that is fine."
This is not breakdown. This is signal. The numbness, the distance, the low-grade management of a life you are not fully inside, that is not who you are. It is what happens when honesty gets too costly for too long. The way back is not more discipline. It is contact.
The passage
Honesty

Drop the performance. Name what is actually happening, the numbness, the fear, the faking. Not to collapse. To finally have something real to stand on.

The "Scared," made conscious
Contact

In a safe, witnessed space: come into real contact with what has been buried. Grief, anger, longing, aliveness. Own it instead of managing it. This is the passage.

The place most men never reach
Devotion

Rebuild relationships, purpose, and presence on honest ground. Act from choice, not fear. Not performance, embodiment. Your life, actually yours.
